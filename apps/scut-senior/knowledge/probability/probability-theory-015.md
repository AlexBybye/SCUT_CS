**八、(12分)** 设总体$X$的密度函数是$f(x;\theta )=\frac {1} {2\theta }{e}^{-\frac {|x|} {\theta }}$，其中$\theta$>0是参数。样本${X}_{1},{X}_{2},...,{X}_{n}$来自总体$X$。

<!-- question: probability-theory-015-Q14 -->

（1）求$\theta$的最大似然估计$\hat{\Theta}_L$；

（2）证明$\hat{\Theta}_L$是$\theta$的无偏估计，且$\hat{\Theta}_L$是$\theta$的相合估计（一致估计）。

**解：**（1）似然函数：$L=\prod _{i=1} ^{n} \frac {1} {2\theta }{e}^{-\frac {|{x}_{i}|} {\theta }}$，$L=\frac {1} {{\left ( {2\theta }\right )}^{n}}{e}^{-\sum _{i=1} ^{n} \frac {|{x}_{i}|} {\theta }}$，（3分）$lnL=-nln(2\theta )-\frac {1} {\theta }\sum _{i=1} ^{n} \left | {{x}_{i}}\right |$  （1分）

$\frac {d} {d\theta }\left ( {lnL}\right )=-\frac {n} {\theta }+\frac {1} {{\theta }^{2}}\sum _{i=1} ^{n} \left | {{x}_{i}}\right |$，令$-\frac {n} {\hat {\theta }}+\frac {1} {{\hat {\theta }}^{2}}\sum _{i=1} ^{n} \left | {{x}_{i}}\right |=0$，（1分）

得

$\hat{\theta}_L=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}|X_i|$  （1分）

（2）$E\left | {X}\right |=\int \frac {1} {\theta }{xe}^{-\frac {x} {\theta }}dx=-\left ( {{xe}^{-\frac {x} {\theta }}}\right ){|}_{0}^{+\infty }+\int {e}^{-\frac {x} {\theta }}dx=-\left ( {{\theta e}^{-\frac {x} {\theta }}}\right ){|}_{0}^{+\infty }=\theta$ （2分）

$EX=\int \frac {1} {2\theta }{xe}^{-\frac {|x|} {\theta }}dx=0$，

$$
\begin {gathered} {EX}^{2}=\int \frac {1} {2\theta }{x}^{2}{e}^{-\frac {|x|} {\theta }}dx=\int \frac {1} {\theta }{x}^{2}{e}^{-\frac {x} {\theta }}dx \\ -\left ( {{x}^{2}{e}^{-\frac {x} {\theta }}}\right ){|}_{0}^{+\infty }+2\int {xe}^{-\frac {x} {\theta }}dx \\ -2\left ( {{x\theta e}^{-\frac {x} {\theta }}}\right ){|}_{0}^{+\infty }+2\theta \int {e}^{-\frac {x} {\theta }}dx=-2\left ( {{\theta }^{2}{e}^{-\frac {x} {\theta }}}\right ){|}_{0}^{+\infty }=2{\theta }^{2} \end {gathered}
$$

![formula-object](assets/probability-theory-015/image-110.png)，$\hat{\Theta}_L$是$\theta$的无偏估计，（1分）

$E{\left | {X}\right |}^{2}={EX}^{2}=2{\theta }^{2}$，

$D\left | {X}\right |={EX}^{2}-{\left ( {E\left | {X}\right |}\right )}^{2}=2{\theta }^{2}-{\theta }^{2}={\theta }^{2}$，$D\left ( {\frac {1} {n}\sum _{i=1} ^{n} \left | {{X}_{i}}\right |}\right )=\frac {D\left ( {\left | {X}\right |}\right )} {n}=\frac {{\theta }^{2}} {n}$

$P\left\{\left|\hat{\theta}_{L}-E\hat{\theta}_{L}\right|>\varepsilon\right\}\leq\frac{\theta^{2}}{n\varepsilon^{2}}\rightarrow0$，$\hat{\Theta}_L$是$\theta$的相合估计   （3分）
